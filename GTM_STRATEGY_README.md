# C-Suite Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy Blueprint

This page renders a pre-filled, executive-level Go-To-Market Strategy assessment for **C-Suite**, focusing on core service offerings, collateral readiness, and prioritized US geographic expansion.

---

## 1. Structure

1. **Executive Header**:
   - Company: **C-Suite**
   - Active Status Badge
   - Quick Print / PDF export action
2. **Section 1: Products / Services**:
   - Comprehensive advisory solutions for **C-Suite** (Executive Strategy & Fractional Leadership, Revenue Operations & GTM Architecture, Board Governance & Compliance, Enterprise Modernization, Strategic M&A Advisory, Executive Search).
   - Prominent **Collaterals – Yes** status badge with verified corporate overview decks, playbooks, portfolios, and engagement MSAs.
3. **Section 2: Geographical Locations (USA Coverage)**:
   - Interactive SVG map of the United States with 5 prioritized territory clusters prominently color-highlighted.
   - Interactive hover tooltips and pin markers with priority ranks directly on the map.
   - **Prioritized Legend Below the Map**:
     - **California, Nevada, Oregon/Seattle** (Priority 1)
     - **North Carolina** (Priority 2)
     - **Texas** (Priority 3)
     - **Florida** (Priority 4)
     - **New York** (Priority 5)
   - Synchronized hover highlighting between the legend cards and the SVG map.
